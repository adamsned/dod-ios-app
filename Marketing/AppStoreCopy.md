## Description (max 4000 chars)

```
Dutch Oven Daddy is the official iOS companion to dutchovendaddy.com — the
cast iron and Dutch oven recipe blog cooking lovers have been bookmarking
for years. Now every recipe is one tap away on your phone, your iPad, and
your kitchen counter.

WHAT'S INSIDE

• Browse every recipe — chronological feed of the latest posts, plus
  category browsing for desserts, beef, chicken, sides, sourdough, and more.
• Search instantly — type a recipe name or ingredient and find your match.
• Save for offline — tap the heart on any recipe to keep it on your device.
  Spotty kitchen Wi-Fi can't break your cook.
• Cook-friendly detail screen — large readable type, tap-to-strike
  ingredient list, numbered steps, embedded video when the recipe has one.
• Related recipes — the bottom of every recipe suggests three more like it.
• Native iPad — sidebar split view, two-column grids, optimized for
  reading while you cook.

WHY THIS APP

We made the iOS app for the same reason we started the blog: cast iron
cooking deserves recipes that work the way you actually cook. No popups,
no ads, no email harvesting. Just recipes.

PRIVACY

No accounts. No tracking. We don't collect personal data. The app sends
anonymous usage data so we can learn which features matter most — and
search queries are cryptographically hashed before they ever leave your
phone.

REQUIREMENTS

iPhone or iPad with iOS 17 or later.

ABOUT DUTCH OVEN DADDY

Dutch Oven Daddy is a cast iron cooking blog at dutchovendaddy.com —
home of the kind of recipes you actually cook on Sunday afternoons.
This app is the easiest way to read them.
```
